Urna Guard

Privacy policy

Extension: Urna Guard (Chrome, Edge and Firefox), version 2.0.0. Effective date: November 1, 2026. The Portuguese version is the reference text.

Summary

Urna Guard does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. All processing happens in your browser, and the extension makes no network connections.

What data is processed and where

DataPurposeWhere it is kept
Text, username, display name and photo (default or not) of posts already shown on your screen on x.com/twitter.comCompute the alert and show the badgeTab memory (discarded when the tab closes)
Flagged posts (username, text excerpt, link, signals and date)Counter, statistics and exports you start yourselfThe browser's storage.local, capped at 1000 items
Settings, trust and watch lists, "not disinformation" feedbackPersonalize the alertsThe browser's storage.local

What the extension does not do

  • It sends no data to any server, the developer's or anyone else's. No telemetry, analytics or ads.
  • It uses no cookies, passwords, tokens or private X APIs.
  • It does not hide or report posts, and takes no action on your account.
  • It loads no remote code.

Permissions

  • storage: keep settings and flagged items locally.
  • Access to x.com and twitter.com: read the posts on screen to show badges. It runs on no other site.

Your rights (Brazil's LGPD, Law 13.709/2018)

Because the data stays only on your device, you have full control over it:

  • Access and portability: "Export JSON/CSV" in the extension popup.
  • Deletion: "Clear data" in the popup or settings. Removing the extension makes the browser delete all of its data.
  • Correction: lists and settings can be edited at any time.

Exported files contain third parties' personal data (public usernames and posts). If you share them, you are responsible: use them only for legitimate purposes such as research or fact-checking, and never for harassment.
